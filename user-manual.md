# Metabolic Nuclear Medicine Platform

## What This App Is

The Metabolic Nuclear Medicine Platform is a clinical record-keeping and monitoring app for physicians, radiologists, and nuclear medicine physicists. It helps care teams at a hospital or clinic track patients receiving metabolic monitoring alongside established, targeted radionuclide therapy (such as Lu-177, Y-90, I-131, or Ra-223 treatment).

**Why use it:** Instead of scattered paper charts or disconnected spreadsheets, a care team gets one shared, secure place to record patient information, imaging, metabolic readings, and treatment history — with every entry logged, every account verified, and every access tracked.

**What this app does not do:** It does not diagnose patients, calculate radiopharmaceutical doses, or recommend treatment. Every clinical decision remains the physician's own, based on their training and their institution's own processes. The app is a support tool, not a decision-maker.

## Getting Started

### For Doctors, Radiologists, and Nuclear Medicine Physicists

1. **Get an invite code from your institution's administrator.** Each hospital or clinic using the app has its own invite code — this is how the app knows which institution's records you should have access to. Ask your institution's admin for it.
2. **Open the app and tap "New here? Create an account."** Enter your name, email, a password, your professional role, and the invite code.
3. **Confirm your email.** You'll receive a confirmation link — tap it, then return to the app.
4. **Sign in.** At this point, your account exists but is not yet approved for full use — you can look around, but you can't yet create or edit patient records.
5. **Wait for admin approval.** Your institution's administrator will verify your account through their own credentialing process. Once approved, you have full access.

### For Institution Administrators

**Getting your institution set up:** Institutions are onboarded directly by the platform team before any staff can join. If your institution isn't set up yet, contact us (see Support, below) to get your institution registered and receive your invite code and founding admin account.

**Approving new staff:** Once you're signed in as an admin, you'll see an **Admin** tab at the bottom of the app. This shows:
- **Pending Approval** — staff who've signed up with your invite code and are waiting for you to verify them. Tap **Verify** once you've confirmed their credentials through your own institution's process.
- **Active Team Members** — everyone currently approved, with the ability to **Deactivate** someone who has left the institution. Deactivating immediately removes all access to patient records — this is different from simply not approving someone, and is meant for staff who are no longer with your institution.
- **Deactivated** — anyone previously deactivated, with the option to **Reactivate** if needed.

**A note on invite codes:** New invite codes (for example, if you suspect your current one has been shared too widely) are currently issued by the platform team, not generated in-app. Contact us if you need a new one.

## Using the App

Once approved, the app organizes everything around individual patients:

- **Patients** — create and search patient records (name, MRN, cancer type/stage). All staff at your institution can see the same patient list — this is a shared care model, not per-doctor silos.
- **Imaging** — from a patient's record, upload scans (photo library or file picker). Standard image files preview directly in the app; DICOM and other specialized files are stored and available for download but viewed on a proper diagnostic workstation, not on a phone screen.
- **Metabolic Monitoring** — log glucose and ketone readings, see trend charts over time, and export a PDF summary.
- **Treatment Log** — record what isotope was administered, at what dose, and by whom it was determined (a dosimetry source reference is always required — the app never calculates a dose itself).
- **Monitoring Dashboard** — an institution-wide view of every active treatment, with the ability to compare pre- and post-treatment imaging side by side.

**If you lose connectivity:** metabolic and treatment log entries you save while offline are queued automatically and sync as soon as you're back online — you'll see a small banner showing how many entries are still pending.

## Security & Privacy

- **Device lock:** the app requires your phone's biometric (Face/Fingerprint) or PIN unlock every time it's opened or returns from the background — a second layer of protection beyond your account login.
- **Institution isolation:** staff at one institution can never see another institution's patient records.
- **Full audit trail:** every time a record is viewed or changed, it's logged — visible to your institution's administrators.
- **Data handling:** designed in alignment with HIPAA and GDPR data-handling principles. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, available from the "Legal" link on the Patients screen.
